You loaded a fresh stack of paper, pressed print, and the printer still says **"Paper Out"**. It is one of the most common printer complaints, and in most cases you can fix it in a few minutes without paying for a repair.

The steps below work for most Epson inkjet and EcoTank printers. Menus and part names differ slightly between models, so check your printer's manual if something looks different.

## Why does the printer say "Paper Out" when there is paper?

The printer decides whether paper is present using a sensor and a pick-up roller. The error usually appears when:

- The paper is not sitting flat or straight in the tray
- The edge guides are too loose or too tight
- A small piece of paper, a label or dust is blocking the paper path
- The feed roller is dirty or worn and cannot grip the sheet
- The paper size or paper source in the print settings does not match what is loaded
- The printer software has a stuck job or a glitch

Start with fix 1 and work down the list. Stop as soon as the printer works.

## Fix 1: Turn the printer off and back on

A power cycle clears many temporary errors.

1. Turn the printer off with its power button.
2. Unplug the power cable from the wall or the printer and wait about 60 seconds.
3. Plug it back in and turn it on.
4. Try printing again.

## Fix 2: Reload the paper properly

Bad loading is the most common cause, so it is worth doing carefully.

1. Remove all the paper from the tray.
2. Fan the stack and tap it on a flat surface so the edges line up. Never mix different sizes or thicknesses.
3. Load the stack so it sits flat against the side of the tray with the printable side facing the way your model requires (usually up for rear trays).
4. Slide the edge guide until it touches the paper snugly. It should not bend the paper.
5. Do not overfill. Keep the stack below the limit mark.
6. Fold out any paper support or extension if your printer has one.

> Do not use curled, damp, very thin or already-printed-on paper. These are common reasons for pick-up problems.

## Fix 3: Check for paper scraps and debris

Even a small torn corner can block the sensor.

1. Turn the printer off and unplug it.
2. Remove the paper and look into the paper feed area with a flashlight.
3. If your model has a rear cover, open it and check the path.
4. Remove any scraps, labels, clips or dust. Use your fingers or tweezers gently. Never use sharp tools and never force anything.
5. Put the covers back and try again.

## Fix 4: Clean the paper feed roller

If the roller is dirty, it slides over the paper instead of pulling it in.

1. With the printer unplugged, find the rubber feed roller that touches the top sheet.
2. Wipe it gently with a lint-free cloth lightly dampened with plain water.
3. Let it dry completely before you reload paper and power on.

Epson publishes an official cleaning procedure for each model. If you have the manual, follow that version.

## Fix 5: Make sure the paper settings match

A mismatch between the software and the real paper can trigger the error.

1. Open the document, choose **Print**, then open **Printer Properties** (or **Preferences**).
2. Check that **Paper Size** (for example A4) matches what is in the tray.
3. Check that **Paper Source** is set to the tray you actually loaded.
4. Print again.

## Fix 6: Clear the print queue and restart the print spooler (Windows)

A stuck job can keep the printer in an error state.

1. Press **Windows + R**, type `services.msc`, and press Enter.
2. Find **Print Spooler**, right-click it, and choose **Stop**.
3. Open File Explorer and go to `C:\Windows\System32\spool\PRINTERS`. Delete the files inside the folder (not the folder itself).
4. Go back to **Services**, right-click **Print Spooler**, and choose **Start**.
5. Print a test page.

## Fix 7: Update or reinstall the printer driver

If the problem started after a Windows update, the driver may be the cause.

1. Open **Settings > Bluetooth & devices > Printers & scanners**, select your printer, and choose **Remove**.
2. Go to the official Epson support website, search for your exact model number, and download the latest driver for your version of Windows.
3. Run the installer and follow the steps.
4. Print a test page.

Always download drivers from the manufacturer's own website to avoid unwanted extras.

## When to call a technician

If all seven fixes fail, the pick-up roller or the paper sensor may be worn or damaged. Those are repairs that need a service center, and for an older low-cost printer the repair cost can be close to the price of a new one. Ask for a quote before you decide.

## How to prevent it

- Store paper flat in a dry place, not in a damp room
- Do not overfill the tray
- Remove paper and cover the printer if it will be unused for weeks
- Use the right paper size and type for each job

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does the error appear after I refill the paper?**
The paper is usually not flat, the guides are too tight, or the stack is touching the sensor. Reload it as in Fix 2.

**Can a firmware or driver update cause this?**
Sometimes a driver update changes default paper settings. Check Fix 5 and Fix 7.

**Is it safe to use compressed air inside the printer?**
Light use around the paper path can help, but avoid blowing dust deeper into the mechanism. Wiping and removing debris by hand is safer.
