At Tech Fix Notes, your privacy matters. This page explains what information is collected when you visit this website and how it is used.

## Information we collect

We do not ask visitors to create accounts. If you send a message through the contact form, we receive the name, email address and message you choose to provide, and we use them only to respond to you.

## Log files and analytics

Like most websites, this site and its hosting provider may collect standard visitor data such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and pages visited. This data is used to keep the site running, understand how it is used, and improve it. It is not used to identify you personally.

## Cookies

Cookies are small files stored on your device. This website and its partners may use cookies to remember preferences, measure traffic and show ads. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not work properly.

## Google AdSense and advertising

This website may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- You can learn more about how Google uses data at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Third-party links

Articles may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

## Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
